Event Technology Services

Audio, video, livestreaming, presentation, and technical event support for community organizations, conferences, religious programs, and private events.

My goal is simple: help organizers deliver reliable events while reducing technical risk and operational stress.

Why Work With Me?

I am a software engineer and long-time technology volunteer with experience designing, documenting, operating, and maintaining audio/video systems for large-scale community programs.

Beyond event operations, I specialize in system design, documentation, training, and process improvement—helping organizations build sustainable technical programs rather than relying on tribal knowledge.
