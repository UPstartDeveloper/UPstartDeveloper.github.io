How It Works

  1. Submit an event inquiry. Tell me about your event, venue, date, attendance, and technical needs.
  2. Discuss requirements. We'll determine what equipment, services, and level of support make sense.
  3. Receive a quote. You'll receive a written scope and price based on your event requirements.
  4. Confirm your booking. Once the date, scope, and payment terms are confirmed, your event is scheduled.
  5. Prepare and execute. We'll handle the agreed-upon setup, technical support, and event execution.

Advance planning is strongly encouraged. Availability for last-minute requests cannot be guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide equipment?

Equipment availability depends on the event, venue, and scope of work. I can work with existing venue/client equipment or help determine what additional equipment is needed.

Can you work with equipment we already have?

Yes. If your venue or organization already has an AV system, I can help configure it, operate it, troubleshoot it, or train your team to use it.

Can you train our volunteers?

Yes. Training, onboarding, documentation, runbooks, and operational checklists are available.

Do you support nonprofit and community organizations?

Limited discounted support may be available for qualifying educational, community, religious, and nonprofit programs.

How far in advance should I book?

Earlier is better, particularly for weddings, conferences, livestreams, and events requiring significant equipment or preparation.

Do you accept last-minute requests?

Last-minute requests may be accommodated when scheduling permits, but availability cannot be guaranteed.

How much do services cost?

Pricing depends on the event type, duration, venue, equipment requirements, and level of technical support. Submit an inquiry for a customized quote.
